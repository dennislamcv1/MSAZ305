Architectural Decision Reflection: GPT-4o vision integration for damage-severity assessment

For damage-severity assessment I weighed three options: prebuilt Azure AI Vision image analysis alone, a custom damage-detection model with rules that convert its detections into a severity grade, and that custom detector paired with GPT-4o-class vision as an advisory reasoning layer. The deciding requirement was that severity is a judgement across several photos and the claim context (vehicle age, loss description, repair estimate), while the Chief Claims Officer needs accuracy that can be measured and adjusters must keep the final decision under NAIC AI-governance expectations. I chose the hybrid because the detector provides per-class precision and recall the business can validate, and GPT-4o-class reasoning, grounded in those detections, produces the overall grade with a rationale an adjuster can check. Had I relied on prebuilt analysis or on the detector with rules, severity would have remained a manual task or a brittle rule set that misses combinations such as minor visible damage with a deployed airbag. Had I relied on GPT-4o alone, I would have given up measurable, reproducible evidence for outputs that are harder to audit and more exposed to model-version retirement.
